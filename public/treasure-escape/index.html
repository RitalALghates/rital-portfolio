Your expedition

Three tasks. One shot at home.

You begin with three hearts. Every decision — how fast you sail, how loud you are — decides whether you make it back.

Collect the treasure

Sail to the abandoned ship, climb aboard and recover the jewels hidden below deck.

Defeat the guardian

Listen for the spider’s warning, return upstairs and land three successful hits.

Escape safely

Return to your boat, follow the escape route and reach the island with at least one heart left.

Three hearts. No saves.

Play a taste

Sneak past the shark

The louder you are, the closer the shark gets. Hold the button to boost — release to glide quietly. Reach the island first.

Press and hold to set sail.

A simplified taste of the noise mechanic — the real game adds a spider, a ship and a shark with its own senses.

Behind the scenes

Built with three tools

Programming language

C#

Scripts for player movement, boat controls, enemy behaviour, missions, health and menus.

Game engine

Unity

Brings scenes and scripts together, handles collisions, lighting and audio, and builds the standalone Windows game.

3D modelling

Blender

The ship, the island and the boat — modelled in Blender and brought into Unity.
